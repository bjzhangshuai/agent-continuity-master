# Business Model

The North Star is Successfully Continued Tasks. Free proves personal cross-device continuity with bounded storage and devices. Pro adds larger history, cross-agent resume and collaboration. Team adds workspace policy, shared Tasks and audit. Enterprise adds SSO/SCIM, BYOK/KMS, residency, private deployment and SLA. Developer pricing can meter accepted events, active agents, storage and egress.

Pricing remains an experiment: measure resume success, weekly continued Tasks, invitation conversion, storage cost and willingness to pay before fixing limits. Marketplace revenue is a long-term option, not the MVP dependency.
